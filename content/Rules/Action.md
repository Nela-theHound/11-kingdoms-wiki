# Action

You may use one action during a round to perform an activity. Common activities include the following: 

- [[Attack]]
- Cast a [[Utility Spell]] 
- [[Concentrate]] 
- [[Defend]]
- [[End an Effect]] 
- [[Find]] 
- [[Help]] 
- [[Hide]] 
- [[Prepare]] 
- [[Reload]] 
- [[Retreat]] 
- [[Rush]] 
- [[Stabilize]]
- [[Use an Item]]
