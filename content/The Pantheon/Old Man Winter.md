# Old Man Winter

Old Man Winter is capricious god who is known for his violent moods causing terrible storms. It's said that the [[Summer Queen]], annoyed by his constant snow storms ruining her lands, bound him in chains and locked him up upon the highest peak of "insert name here". There his anger builds up until he can bring about cold that will disrupt the entire land. The [[Summer Queen]] makes a pilgrimage there every year to quell his anger, which is how the seasons of summer and winter are said to have come about.

Old Man Winter is typically only worshipped by those who live in the coldest areas, such as those that reside on mountains or in the far north. Commonly offerings of winter berries are left for him but it someone is about to make a long trek they might sacrifice a goat to him in hopes of appeasing his anger so that they won't experience any blizzards along the way.
