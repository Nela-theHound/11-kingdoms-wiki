A now dead and forgotten god who's death created the [[Gulf of Asteron]]. He came to the world to lead his struggling people, creating miracles, curing plagues, growing crops and much more. As time went on however cracks started to show, he would only bless those who followed his faith and villagers, fearing they might offend him or that he would revoke his blessing, began turning against their neighbors if they were non believers. This escalated into witch hunts where dissatisfied followers would attempt to seek out non believers who they believed were the cause of any ills that befell them. The other gods took notice of the tipping balance and put an end to the God's reign however the war that took place killed many and when the God perished his body exploded causing the deaths of all who lived in the city he ruled over and the creation of the [[Gulf of Asteron]].
