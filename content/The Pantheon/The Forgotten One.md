A now dead and forgotten god who's death created the [[Gulf of Asteron]]. He came to the world to lead his struggling people, creating miracles, curing plagues, growing crops and much more. As time went on however cracks started to show, he would only bless those who followed his faith and villagers, fearing they might offend him or that he would revoke his blessing, began turning against their neighbors if they were non believers. This escalated into witch hunts where dissatisfied followers would attempt to seek out non believers who they believed were the cause of any ills that befell them. The other gods took notice of the tipping balance and put an end to the God's reign however the civil war that took place killed many and when the God perished his body exploded causing the deaths of all who lived in the city he ruled over and the creation of the [[Gulf of Asteron]]. Some say his death cause a tear is space leading to the [[Void]] and that this tear still sits open at the site of his death 2,000 years later.

Was the god of the cosmos, and together with [[Sister Sun]] created humanity. His symbol was a shining star and his doctrine was all about forging new frontiers and following the North Star to find your way. His domains were that of the cosmos, the night sky, mysteries, and fire. He was said to have given humanity the spark of life that ignited the fire humanity then learned to harness.

After his death the gods and those who had been affected by the war chose to erase his presence from the world and now his name is forgotten to time. Most don't even know that he helped [[Sister Sun]] create humanity. Although there is a small movement that most consider a cult that believe he will be reborn and rise again to rule over all of humanity.
