# Summer Queen

The Summer Queen is said to be the first of the Fey. She created all of the Fey species and ruled over them for a time before turning the crown over to her first daughter. While the Summer Queen is not as indiscriminately cruel like [[Old Man Winter]] is, she is still feared by those who are not Fey. The Summer Queen preferred her own creations and saw the species of the other [[gods]] as lesser, instilling that ideology into the Fey. Because of this the Fey see the other races as below them, their views ranging between seeing them as cute pets to bugs that could easily be crushed under foot, either way the other races are wary of crossing the paths of Fey.

The Summer Queen is worshipped by Fey. Instead of temples dedicated to her, the Fey show their faith by hosting parties in her name and sharing in the revelry and abundance she has provided them. Lesser Fey show their faith by playing tricks on the other races.
