# Old Lady Death

The oldest of the gods, Old Lady Death created [[Father Sky]], [[World Mother]], [[Old Man Winter]], and the [[Summer Queen]]. She is depicted as an old woman who brings comfort to the recently departed as a grandmother might welcome home a child she has not seen in years. Worship of her is incredibly common as she is both the progenitor of many of the other gods of the world and because all those living will eventually face death so no matter where you are in [[Ettia]] you can find shrines, altars, and temples dedicated to Old Lady Death. Commonly food and flowers are left as offerings and the dead are typically buried with a trinket to give to Old Lady Death when they pass through her domain.

Old Lady Death is known for her love of shiny objects and as such birds such as magpies, crows, and ravens are all associated with her and many believe that those birds ferry souls to her domain along with the trinkets they collect.
