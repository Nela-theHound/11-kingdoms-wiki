# Level 1 Rogue

**Attributes:** Increase two by 1.

**Characteristics:** Health +3

**Languages and Professions:** You either add one language to the list of ones you can speak or add one common, criminal, or wilderness profession.

**Nimble Recovery:** You can use an action to heal damage equal to your healing rate and then move up to half your Speed without triggering free attacks. Once you use this talent you cannot use it again until you finish a rest.

**Trickery:** Once per round, you can make an attack roll or challenge roll with 1 boon. If you attack with 1 boon from this talent your attack deals 1d6 extra damage.

---

# Level 2 Rogue

**Characteristics:** Health +3

**Exploit Opportunity:** Once per round, when the total of your attack roll is 20 or higher and exceeds the target number by at least 5, you can take another turn at any point before the end of the round.

**Roguery Talent:** Choose a roguery talent from the ones described below.

| Magic      | Increase your Power by 1 and discover one [[Tradition]]. Then make two choices, you either discover another tradition or learn one spell from a tradition you know. If you take this talent again you make the attack roll with 1 boon and can affect a creature that doesn't understand what you say. |

If you take this talent again your weapon attacks deal 1d6 extra damage to targets frighted in this way.                            |

# Level 5 Expert Rogue

**Characteristics:** Health +3

**Dirty Tricks:** Your attacks deal 1d6 extra damage when you make an attack roll with 1 boon.

**Rogue Cunning:** You can use Trickery twice per round.
