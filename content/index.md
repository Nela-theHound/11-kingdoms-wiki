Welcome to The 11 Kingdoms Wiki! Here you can find information about the lore of the world of [[Ettia]], information about playable races (called [[Ancestries]]) and classes (called [[Path]]s) and more! This site is still a work in progress so pages will be updated overtime. Please leave feedback on the t11k lore channel in discord.

Check out [[The Main Premise]] to learn about what the campaign is about. If you're curious about the various gods and religions of the world check out [[Gods]].

Building a character? Head over to [[Character Creation]] to learn how. Check [[Ancestries]] to see what you can play as or [[Path]] to see what classes/paths you can choose from. When you've decided on you Path, you might need to check [[Tradition]]s to learn about the different traditions of magic that are available to learn.

Want to learn something about the system Shadow of the Demon Lord? See the [[Rules Index]]
for a list of game rules, or click on the associated links.
