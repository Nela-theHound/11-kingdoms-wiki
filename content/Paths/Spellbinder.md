# Level 3 Spellbinder

**Attributes:** Increase 2 by 1

**Characteristics:** Health +3, Power +1

**Magic:** Either learn a new tradition or learn 1 spell. In addition learn the *Spellbound Weapon* spell described below.

#### Spellbound Weapon - Spellbinder utility 0
You touch one weapon you can reach and imbue it with magical powers that last for 4 hours. Until the spell ends, you gain the following benefits with the target weapon.

- The weapon counts as your implement
- The weapon grants you 1 boon on attack rolls made with it.
- If the weapon is within 1 mile of you, you can use an action to teleport it to your hand or to your feet if your hands are full
- You can use an action to touch the target weapon and remove all damage from it, even if the weapon is destroyed, provided you have a fragment of it.

**Sacrifice:** You can use a triggered action to expend a casting of this spell. Until the end of the round, your attacks with this weapon deal extra damage equal to your Power.

# Level 6 Spellbinder

**Characteristics:** Health +3

**Magic:** Either learn a new tradition or learn 1 spell.

**Invest Power:** You can use an action, or a triggered action on your turn, to invest greater power into the target weapon of your spellbound weapon spell. You touch the weapon and expend the casting of a rank 1 spell or higher. For 1 minute eldritch flames dance across the weapon lighting the area within 10 yards of it. While the flames are lit your attacks with the weapon deal 1dd6 extra damage.

# Level 9 Master Spellbinder

**Characteristics:** Health +3, Power +1

**Magic:** Either learn a new tradition or learn 1 spell.

**Magic Weapon:** When you attack with your spellbound weapon, you make the attack roll with 1 boon and deal 1d6 extra damage.
