# Revenants

Sometimes death calls too soon, claiming the soul before a mortal can fulfill a destiny, right a wrong, or repay a debt. When the burden of responsibility proves too heavy to set aside, death may stay its hand and let the mortal complete those unfinished tasks of a revenant.

**Optional Rule: Rising From the Dead:**
The GM might allow a dead character to return as a Revenant, provided they meet the criteria and doing so would be appropriate to the story. 

---

# Creating a Revenant

First create a character with an ancestry that is mortal and living, possesses a soul, and was not created by magic - so no Fae, Clockworks, or Elementals. Then apply the following traits to that character.

**Starting Attribute Scores:** [[Strength]] +1, [[Intellect]] -1

**Characteristics:** Defense -1, Insanity +1d3

---

## Level 4 Expert Revenant

**Characteristics:** Health +6

You either learn one spell, gain Eyes of the Underworld, or gain an expert trait from your other ancestry.

**Eyes of the Underworld:** You can use an action to make a [[Will]] attack roll against the [[Will]] of one target creature within short range that can see you. On a success, the creature is frightened for 1 minute. On a failure the creature is immune to your use of this talent until it completes a rest.
