# Professions

Professions are occupations, pursuits, and areas of knowledge, broadly conceived to give you all the room you need to define your character in concert with the other descriptive elements gained during character creation. Thus, it’s up to you to decide what your character did within the profession, how your character gained it, and what it means in the context of other information you learned about your character. 

**Starting Professions:** You begin with two professions. You can choose any profession you like or let the dice decide. In the latter case, roll a d6 and consult the Profession Types table, following the instructions. 

**Languages:** You can trade out a profession to learn to speak another language or gain the ability to read a language you already know how to speak.

## Profession Types

| d6  | Profession                                                                     |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | **Academic**. You are a scholar, you can read a write a language you can speak |
| 2   | **Common**. You work in a trade.                                               |
| 3   | **Criminal**. You engage in illicit or illegal activities.                     |
| 4   | **Martial**. You joined the army, law enforcement, or local militia.           |
| 5   | **Religious**. You belong to a religious institution.                          |
| 6   | **Wilderness**. You live beyond civilization's bounds.                         |
