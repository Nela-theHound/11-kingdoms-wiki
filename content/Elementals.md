# Lore of the Elementals

Each elemental's home island contains an “elemental heart” which is a giant [[Mana]] crystal that is attuned to a particular element. For example the [[Salamanders]]'s elemental heart was forged in the heart of a volcano and contains the element of fire. The [[Hamadryads]]’s heart is at the core of a great tree. The [[Sylphs]]'s heart crystalized from the water vapors of the clouds at the top of their mountains. And the [[Undines]]’s heart took shape under the waters of the great sea.
