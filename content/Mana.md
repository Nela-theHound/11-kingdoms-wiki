# Lore of Mana and Magic

Mana, from which all magic power is derived, exists ambiently in the world. Mana is a byproduct of the [[gods]] and so when the gods created the world, created creatures in the world, or perform any miracles, mana is created. There is a small amount of mana in all things, from plant life, to the air itself and magic users draw on both innate power within themselves as well as the ambient mana around them to cast spells. A magic user will have a reservoir of power within them that is determined by their own magical capacity, that they use to cast spells, when that reservoir runs dry however the ambient mana around them fills it back up as they rest. Typically within a 12 hour period most magic users will have fully replenished their reservoir. 

Mana also occasionally concentrates in one area. Such examples are mana stones, which are highly concentrated pure mana that has crystalized, or the [[elementals]] who were born from areas with high mana concentration.
