**Dark Magic Traditions:** Certain traditions reveal fearful secrets known as dark magic. You gain 1 Corruption each time you discover a dark magic tradition. As well, each time you learn a spell from a dark magic tradition, roll a d6. If the number is less than the total number of dark magic spells you have learned, you gain 1 Corruption. 

However, each dark magic spell you learn grants 1 boon on challenge rolls you make to avoid gaining Insanity
