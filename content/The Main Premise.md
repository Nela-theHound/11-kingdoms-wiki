The 11 Kingdoms is a story about the awakening of a [[Dark Lord]] who tried to become a god 1,000 years ago. He was stopped by a group of adventurers, one from each kingdom, who came together each with the power of their [[god]]s to stop him. After [[The Calamity]] the heroes hid away the [[artifacts]] the gods had given them, knowing that their power was too great for any kingdom to possesses. Now 1,000 years later, an archeological dig has unearthed the tomb of the [[Dark Lord]] and an apprentice found his circlet which held his soul, causing the [[Dark Lord]]'s soul to enter the apprentice and become active once again. Now the [[The Magic and Archeological Institute]] has put forth a call for adventurers to help find the [[artifacts]] that once defeated him and once again put an end to his reign.
