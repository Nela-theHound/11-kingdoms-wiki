The world is divided into 11 Kingdoms. In the East Humanity has spread to every corner of the continent and both the Main Human Kingdom and The Human/Dwarf Kingdom are leading the charge in the technomancy revolution. Supplied with magic stones and raw metals from the Orc Kingdom, Humans and Dwarves have been working to create technology powered by magic such as [[Clockworks]], trains, airships and factories. 

## Elemental Lands

On the Western side of the world lie the Islands that birthed the [[Elementals]] [[Hamadryads]], [[Undines]], [[Sylphs]], and [[Salamanders]]. The Hamadryads and Undines live harmoniously on the Western Isle while both being in conflict with the Salamanders who live on the Volcanic Easter Isle. On the Northern Isle the Sylphs mainly keep to themselves in high mountains that most cannot traverse.

## Summer Isles

To the South of the Elemental Isle lies hidden the Summer Isles, where the Fae reside. After their second queen disappeared following the first emergence of the [[Dark Lord]] they decided to cut their lands off from those who they see as lesser races. There are ways of entering and leaving Faerie but it is a secret known only to the Fae. 

The Isles themselves are a beautiful land full of natural wonders. Snow capped mountains, clear blue lakes surrounded by lush forests, and fields of wild flowers of every color. The immortal [[Elves]] are typically happy to spend their lives enjoying the beauty of the land through various art forms. They hold month long feasts and in general care only about seeking out pleasurable things.

Though most [[Elves]] choose to stay on their Isle, some Fae leave their homeland for various reasons. The most common Fae to be found in the world are [[Goblins]], for they were exiled after their queen stole from the Fae queen. Some say the Goblin queen stole a kiss, other say she stole a gem, while still some stories say she kissed the Fae queen to distract her from the theft of a jewel. Regardless the [[Goblins]] were stripped of their immortality and sent to live with the rest of the mortals. [[Changelings]] are also unwelcome in the Summer Isles as they are seen as nothing more than dolls made of sticks and paper used to steal mortal children, and if they survive to adulthood they are left to fend for themselves. [[Pixies]] also commonly pass over into the mortal world so that they might have some fun playing tricks on unsuspecting mortals since the Fae in the Summer Isles are wise to their tricks. Some Pixies enjoy playing harmless pranks, like stealing one sock from a pair, while others play more nefarious pranks that cause harm.

In recent years a consortium of various Fae who were abandoned by the [[Elves]] or simply chose to leave the Summer Isles have created their own Kingdom on the very southern tip of the main continent.
